Welcome to Doskvol, a Victorian-era city of perpetual night, where electric lights illuminate cobblestone streets, gondolas navigate winding canals, and fortunes are made and lost behind the doors of elegant townhouses.

Imagine a haunted industrial world of steam engines, clockwork, alchemy and strange electrical devices. Electroplasm, refined from the blood of monstrous leviathans, powers much of the city’s remarkable technology. Ghosts are real, the invisible ghost field overlaps the physical world, and occult specialists can interact with forces most sensible people avoid.

You’re members of an up-and-coming criminal crew operating out of Silkshore, a district of artists, entertainers and questionable establishments. Your lair, the shuttered Clermont Lyric Opera House, is *officially* closed for safety violations. 

It's time to make a name for yourselves.
