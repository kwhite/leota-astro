Back in 2003, this gaming group first got together. It was Buffy, and it was pretty epic. And somehow, in 2025-2026, between Jackie and Kat, we managed to restore most of our episode summary work on the campaign. We had playlists, secondary web presences for towns, colleges, covens and more, as well as the birth of the infamous quote napkin. And... musical episodes.

Needless to say, we had a lot more spare time back then.

*(I also can't help noting that rereading all of this, our characters tended to get naked, chemically influenced, and/or laid a lot more when we were in our 20s.)*

*(See, now you totally want to read these summaries.)*
