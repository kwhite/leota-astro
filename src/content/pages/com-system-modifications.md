**Action yes/no? Does degree of success matter?**

Multiple actions require more points. Tags = success points.

<ul><li><strong>2 points</strong> = create a tag</li><li><strong>1 point</strong> = change of level or #</li><li><em>e.g. defending while attacking</em></li></ul>

<h3 id="complication-examples">Complication Examples</h3>

<ol><li>Climbing a slippery ladder</li><li>Drop something important</li><li>Get spotted when sneaking</li><li>Take too long when time matters</li><li>Ladder breaks, so next person can’t use it</li></ol>

<hr>

<h3 id="what-can-i-do-with-success-points">What Can I Do With Success Points?</h3>

<ul><li><strong>2 pts</strong> — Create an item/condition anyone could use (or out of your control)</li><li><strong>1 pt / alteration:</strong><ul><li><strong>Affect:</strong> physical or mental stat (or both) [Block]</li><li><strong>Area of effect:</strong> person → room → house → apartment</li><li><strong>Size of group:</strong> person → ~4 → 10–15 → 30 → lots</li><li><strong>Prolong/repeating:</strong> team → squad → platoon → company → whole [ward?]</li><li>Other tags as agreed on</li></ul></li><li><strong>1 clue / pt</strong></li></ul>

> *Climb while dodging bats = -2 to shoot a gun*
