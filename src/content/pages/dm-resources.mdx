I'm a very lazy DM. Like very. Lazy. That's why I like open-world campaigns; less work for me, more work for my players.

As it happens, there are a lot of amazing folx on the internet who are not as lazy as I am, and they are my enablers. This page is a few of the fantastic resources I reference the most often, as well as some specialty ones I don't want to forget about. These things may help you if you're operating in my world (and under my constraints).
