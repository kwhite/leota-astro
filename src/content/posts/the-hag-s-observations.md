The Night Hag had some choice taunting prophetic-sounding commentary for each of you. Here they are, in case you missed them.

Erash

> Child of flame and child of gold... Your family fate in legends told… if the right people learn your name… Wildemount could go up in flame.” She laughs wildly to herself. Delightful, little firefly.

Ferric

> Hmmm. Your brand of idealism makes me itchy. What would that overgrown lizard of yours think of this questionable company you’re keeping? Maybe the doubters back home are right. Maybe you can’t protect the weak. Maybe you just get all those weak and unwise idealists killed in the misplaced name of justice. In that case, your new friends should run while they still can.

Khoraka

> Enchanting. I do love a good fall from grace. Withered wings, withered hands, withered hopes. Just delightful.“ She gestures to his whip. ”We really ought to milk those fangs of yours, though. You have no idea who you’re dealing with, do you? But at least the self-destruction will be fun to watch. Fun for you too, I expect, as you seem like the type who enjoys that sort of thing.

> She is closer than you think. And what will you do when she comes for you, I wonder?

Snow

> Aaah, the wayward daughter, back from the corrupting influence of real civilization.” She eyes Snow’s bow and smiles smugly. “So that’s where it went. Don’t you remember what they told you about curiosity and cats?” She smiles a wicked little smile. “But that’s the least of your worries. You think you have no stake in this war? They say blood is thicker than water, but they both run easy in my experience. He’s not what you think he is. Never was. And now his only agenda is the queen’s.
