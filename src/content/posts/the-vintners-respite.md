<blockquote><em>“A place to be forgotten, remembered only by the taste of firefruit on your tongue.”</em><br>— <em>Traveler’s Guide to Kamordah</em></blockquote>

**Location:**  
Tucked into a hollowed basalt rise on the southern slope of Kamordah, this cosy B&B style inn is styled with ivy-dripped walls and volcanic glass inlaid in the floor. The inn overlooks fields of crimson ash-blossoms and the shadowed town below.

**Proprietor:  
Mirta Bramblehook** *(Female Lightfoot Halfling)*  
A retired adventurer-turned-vintner with a sharp tongue and a long memory. Mirta knows every rumor in Kamordah and curates her inn like a fine vintage: warm, quiet, and not for the faint of palate.

<blockquote>“You didn’t hear it from me, love—but the courier with the amber satchel left in the dead of night. Checked out without breakfast. <em>Without breakfast.</em> Now what sort of honest person does that?”</blockquote>
