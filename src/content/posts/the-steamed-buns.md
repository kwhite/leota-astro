<blockquote><em>“Our steam lifts more than aches. It lifts masks.”</em> — Crystal Waters</blockquote>

Tucked above the aromatic bustle of a busy bakery in Zadash’s beating heart, **The Steamed Buns** is more than a bathhouse — it is a whispered promise of indulgence, secrecy, and sublime rejuvenation. A spiral staircase, barely noticeable behind the bakery’s ovens, leads to an unmarked brass door. Beyond lies a hidden haven of heat and hedonism: mosaic-tiled baths shimmer with scented steam, silk-draped massage rooms glow with candlelight, and the air carries hints of lavender, cardamom, and secrets.

The proprietress, **Crystal Waters**, is a dusky tiefling with gold-flecked eyes and a laugh like warm wine. She’s rumored to know the desires of her clients before they speak them. Under her velvet rule, the spa is staffed entirely by tieflings — each exquisitely trained in the healing arts, flirtation, conversation, and discretion. Here, scars are softened, burdens forgotten, and information traded like perfumes.

Whether you’re a visiting merchant, cloaked noble, tired adventurer, or someone needing to disappear for a few hours, **The Steamed Buns** has a treatment to match your need — or your cover story.
