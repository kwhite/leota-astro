## In the arena

- We pick up where we left off, fighting a group of red slaads.
- Fortunately, we learned not to let them strike with their claws before it happened.
- It took us a while, but one by one they fell.
- The crowd loves us!
- They started chanting, *“Ig! Ig! Ig! Ig!”*
- Then… we met Ig, a gigantic magma elemental. And fought it. And somehow didn’t die. Mostly because we kept Ig out of the lava successfully. Go us!
- In fighting Ig, we learned that the portal is protected by a barrier; we can’t just jump in (or drop Ig in, either).
- We end with Ig literally dead at the feet of the city’s ruler, Faz’hul al-Tasim, who rises to address us…
