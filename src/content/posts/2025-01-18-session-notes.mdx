### Our questions

We had five questions we could ask or items we could find in the White Rabbit’s Enclave thanks to Daniel. Here’s what we did with them.

> **How do we get IN to the Gingerbread House Enclave?**

- Candy is a wish fulfillment drug that acts as a key to get past the veil and into the enclave without permission from the owner.
- Just remember that you could well end up in a cage and pushed into a furnace.
- She probably has something or someone that is waiting for Rifts who enter her Enclave this way.

> **How do we safely use distilled Rift energy?**

- Distilling Rift energy is a dangerous form of magic that has been attempted many times.
- The idea behind it is that a Rift can be stripped of their magic and someone else can then use it.
- Legends from people who claim to have accomplished this feat say that anyone can use the magic but to truly unite with the magic, your own Rift has to agree to it.
- Having a Rift and using a vial of Rift tincture is said to be powerful but dangerous because it means you embody two Rifts at once.

The research we read contains references to *Alchemy of the Soul* but we’d have to start referencing other materials/asking additional questions to get to that.

> **How do we protect ourselves from the distillation process?**

- Stay out of the furnace if you don’t want to be distilled.
- The furnace was a tool invented by **Rosina Leckermaul** — drug trafficker who worked with a cult called “**Magnum Opus**.”
- She has disappeared off the scene lately. Rosina is now behaving “like an old lady, not like a powerful witch.” Like she’s “lost the spark” of something.

> **How do we get OUT of the Gingerbread House Enclave?**

- The Gingerbread House is considered a particularly dangerous Enclave.
- The Enclave is controlled by the Rift of the Gingerbread Witch.
- She knows who is in and who is not.
- She opens the door to let people in and to let people out.
- Whoever controls this Rift controls the Enclave.
- The only known way out is: she lets you out, go through the furnace, or hijack the tincture (since we are not dealing with the real GB witch… we think).

> **Found item: runic chicken bones**

And now we have…. A chicken bone. More accurately, a jar of chicken bones bearing runic inscriptions.
