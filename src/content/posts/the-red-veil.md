<blockquote class="kg-blockquote-alt"><em>“One sip to loosen the tongue. One song to stir the soul.”</em></blockquote>

Perched on a blackstone terrace overlooking Kamordah’s smoldering vineyards, **The Red Veil** occupies a vine-draped manor partially carved into the cliffside. Crimson velvet curtains mask tall arched windows. Firelight flickers off obsidian goblets and glass decanters suspended in levitating rings above the bar.

- Tables of dark wood and volcanic glass circle a **sunken stage**, enchanted to reflect moods with ambient color and swirling mist.
- Each evening begins with a **tasting flight**, a **rotating chef’s menu**, and live entertainment—songs, poetry, illusion, or intimate theater.
- The club enforces a **"soft masks encouraged"** policy. Many guests wear stylish half-masks or veils in keeping with the name.

<h2 id="proprietor">Proprietor</h2>

***Veleris Ormelle (Female Half-Elf)***

Veleris is poised, persuasive, and partial to tragic songs and sweet dessert wines. Her reputation is one of discretion—her memory, inconveniently perfect.

<blockquote>“Everyone wears a mask here, darling. Some are just made of better porcelain.”</blockquote>
