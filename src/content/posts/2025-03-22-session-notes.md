## Nicodranas

<figure class="kg-card kg-image-card kg-card-hascaption"><a href="/assets/images/2025/03/image.png"><img src="/assets/images/2025/03/image.png" class="kg-image" alt="" loading="lazy" width="1456" height="816"></a><figcaption>Nicodranas</figcaption></figure>

- The party travels to Nicodranas to secure passage to Xarzith Kitril.
- Khoraka negotiates passage for them on a schooner named the *Quick Fortune*.
- They do some shopping, relaxing, and carousing before spending the night at an inn in the city.
- The next morning they board the *Quick Fortune*, moving further out to sea rather than hugging the coast to avoid the stronger swells.

## The *Quick Fortune*

<figure class="kg-card kg-image-card kg-card-hascaption"><a href="/assets/images/2025/03/IMG_0568-1.png"><img src="/assets/images/2025/03/IMG_0568-1.png" class="kg-image" alt="" loading="lazy" width="1456" height="816"></a><figcaption>The <i><em class="italic" style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Quick Fortune</em></i></figcaption></figure>

- Ship life is ship life.
- The lookout sees a storm but the captain doesn’t anticipate problems.
- Turns out he’s wrong and the ship gets caught in a raging storm on the open ocean.
- In the aftermath, the captain realizes the storm has pushed the ship out towards Inkclaw Reef, near Urukayxl and the Swavain Islands.
- Another ship has run aground on the reef, and smoke rises from the recent wreckage.
- The *Quick Fortune* approaches the wreck to offer assistance to any survivors.
