Floating through the roiling impermanent chaos of Limbo using only their thoughts, the Brightbound make their way to a large geode-like asteroid with a cavern entrance. Once inside, gravity and the laws of matter and energy reassert themselves.

## The Geode Caverns

![](/assets/images/2025/12/IMG_9128-1.jpeg)

<Callout color="yellow" emoji="💡">

For fun, I will use the Brightbound party members as the player names below.

</Callout>

- Cypher scouts ahead at all the major intersections.
- The party works their way down a giant hole in the ground and continues to search.
- Faced with a wall bisecting a diamond slab with ruby eyes embedded at either end, the party messes with stuff (okay, Kali messes with stuff) and ends up having to fight a shade of Phelan that emerges from the wall.
- Touching the eye teleports the person to the other side of the wall.
- They then face a set of diamond slab doors with eyes instead of handles; these also respond to touch, and the party teleports into a large, crystal-rimmed room where Vecna stands, looking upward, concentrating on a glowing orb of expanding energy in front of him.

## At the end of all things

![](/assets/images/2025/12/IMG_1608.jpeg)

> Impossible! You were defeated. Here. Mere moments ago… But there is a look in your eye that is… different. You are not who you once were. RAVEN QUEEN. You meddle in things beyond you, and you will pay for your insolence!

- Cypher immediately goes on the attack.
- Zerus and Kali team up and chase a teleporting Vecna around the room.
- Xhalara and Elarion cast in support, and are frequently Counterspelled By the arch-lich, but are still able to buff and aid the party.
- With a final mighty flurry of blows, Zerus brings Vecna to his (temporary) end.
- Xhalara offers a Greater Divine Intervention — and Wishes that Vecna never returns. The Everlight speaks through her, saying that while she cannot forbid his return, she can forestall it. Rather than rising in 1-100 years, It will be 200 years before Vecna returns to this plane.
- As the Brightbound look to one another in victory, reality disperses, and the FGC feel themselves lifted away, as the lifeless bodies of their borrowed heroes fall softly to the ground.

## Back to Life

![](/assets/images/2025/12/IMG_0831-1.jpeg)

- In the quiet darkness, the Raven Queen looms in front of the FGC and graces them with her promised epic favor, acknowledging them for a job well done.
- The soft, enveloping nothingness ends in static and blinding pain, as Erash, Ferric, Khoraka, Phelan, and Snow awake in a cryptlike room where the Azers had hidden their bodies.
- Phelan hands out healing potions that take the edge off, at which point they realize Valgara is not with them…
