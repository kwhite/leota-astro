### Scene Notes

- Wainright appears and takes Rachel (the Gingerbread Witch) to task for running behind.
- Rachel complains that the pace of production is unrealistic.
- They take their argument to a back room.
- There’s a big fight with the gingerbread men, in which one of the other abductees gets them to chase her.
- Geoffrey manages to break out of his cage by using the magic of the forge to destroy it, but it is a slow and dangerous process.
- We use the forge to break out one small girl as well; she speaks no English but comes with the group as they explore deeper into the building looking for a way out.
