### About Kalfu

The Lwa most closely associated with chaos in Haitian Vodou is [Kalfu](https://ahavacelestialorder.org/kalfu-the-loa-of-crossroads-and-chaos-in-haitian-vodou/) (also spelled Carrefour). As the Lwa of the crossroads at night, Kalfu controls access to both good and malevolent spirits and governs the darker, more unpredictable forces of the spiritual world.

> Kalfu embodies disorder, misfortune, and the volatile nature of fate. While his counterpart, Papa Legba, opens safe and clear pathways, Kalfu holds sway over the chaotic and dangerous routes, making him a figure often approached with caution. He is seen as a master of chance, magic, and the unseen energies that can bring either fortune or disaster.

> In Vodou ceremonies, Kalfu is often offered strong rum, sometimes laced with gunpowder, reflecting his volatile and explosive nature. His colors are usually red and black, symbolizing both power and danger.
