## When last we met…
The crew and their allies, Maria and Fiona, had successfully extricated a severely injured Erica Garza from the clutches of the now-dead Jakub Grund, a sadistic serial killer who made his home deep in The Warrens of the Undertrash. They are finding their way back out of the darkness, with an unconscious Erica carried in Cillian’s arms.

## Out of the Warrens
![Reinforcements](/assets/images/2026/11/reinforcements.png)
> Wren: “So you can’t remember if you slept with her?”<br />
> John: “I mean… uh, that’s fair.”

Two very bored-looking women wearing cloaks that look like feathers lean against the walls, gossiping in Londonese. One is buffing their nails. The other one is knitting a sock, needles glinting in the electric lights. Their conversation is lively and engaged, and not at all appropriate to their trash-filled surroundings. The group emerges, and John catches sight of the duo. These must be the reinforcements sent by Isabella. The blonde looks… vaguely familiar, but he can’t quite place her. She is, however, *very* happy to see him.

> London: “I am very educated about certain things. That does not make me very smart.”

The women introduce themselves as Allison and Quilla, and London recognizes Quechua patterns and cultural details in Quilla’s accent and clothing. Their boss’s instructions are to bring the crew somewhere safe.

As soon as the crew is clear of the Warrens, their phones start chiming and buzzing. Cillian has a message from Cyrus, which Maeve helps him answer since his arms are full of unconscious luchadora. Kevin has a voicemail from Denver’s hospice. 

She has taken a turn for the worse, and he should come now if he wants to say anything to her. 

The gut response is that everyone wants to go with him. Quilla and Allison point out that an entire group of wanted people arriving together might cause problems for everyone involved, Denver included. Isabella has arranged somewhere for them to hide, with food, sleeping space, and hot showers. After some consideration, Kevin and Wren head for Denver, while London, John, and Geoffrey accept the shelter. 

> London: “Does this place involve hot showers? Because if it involves hot showers, I don’t really care where it is.”

Sergei, still elsewhere with Daniel, receives an anxious, rambling text from Michelle asking whether he is all right and whether messaging him might get him into trouble. They have a preciously awkward text conversation (that none of the rest of us are privy to). Messages from the returning crew follow, and Daniel and Sergei arrange to join them elsewhere in the Undertrash before heading to Isabella’s safe house. Sergei intends to slip away to Helix afterward. 

Marcus meets up with the group and offers quick access to a hospital that provides a maximum standard of care and a minimum standard of questions. Ultimately, the group agrees to send Erica with him and Maria, who assures them that she will keep Erica safe. Arms finally free, Cillian takes his phone from Maeve to call Cyrus and steps away from the crowd.

> Cillian: “Apparently my mother is taking up arms against me, which is… new.”

Cillian returns from his call with the troubling news that his mother has put a target on his back, for reasons he doesn’t quite understand. But Cyrus reassures him he can finally explain things he previously couldn’t share, and Cillian invites Maeve along to Avalon. She is excited to go there with him now that he can fully understand what he is seeing — and who his friends really are. There have been whole conversations full of subtext he couldn’t follow, and things she couldn’t tell him because he would forget, explain them away, or decide she was crazy. 

> London: “Just… Maeve, if you are going, be careful.”<br />
> Cillian: “Whoa, whoa, whoa, We're going to Avalon. I'm not taking her to Parkside.”<br />
> Maeve: “Parkside? What's in Parkside? That's where Briana… OH, you know, that makes so much sense. OF COURSE. Um. Sorry. I'll be quiet. I mean, I won't *really* be quiet, but I can be quiet for at *least* a few minutes.”

Maeve texts Cyrus a quick heads-up that Cillian “knows everything” at this point. Cyrus responds with relief, but not surprise. Quilla gives the two of them directions since they’re headed in a different direction, and they prepare to part ways from the rest of the group.

> Cillian: “This is actually a relationship if you go to this conversation. I'm just warning you right now.”<br />
> Maeve: “I… okay? That was… abrupt.”

## The Safe “House”
Isabella’s safe house is an attractive, soaring downtown office building between tenants. One company has moved out, and renovations for the next won’t begin for a couple of weeks. There is some furniture, sleeping bags, air mattresses, and a substantial selection of takeout collected from deliveries to different offices. Quilla and Allison haven’t been briefed on the entire situation; they know John is wanted and can make a reasonable deduction about the people hiding with him, but their instructions were simply to collect the crew. After they arrive and get oriented, London discovers a stocked margarita machine off to one side. While he’s not a drinker, it’s a great sign for the rest of the crew.

## Sweet Chariot Hospice
At the hospice, Kevin and Wren find Denver resting and unresponsive in a small, serviceable room that smells of antiseptic. The staff clearly care, but the place doesn’t have much money. B is present when they arrive, holding Denver’s hand. Kevin apologizes for being away, but B is just glad to see him. Nobody can give him a useful answer about how much time Denver has left, other than “not much.” Wren asks to hold her hand too, and B suggests a group prayer. They have a moment of prayer and personal silence, and B takes her leave to give them some time alone with Denver. As she says goodbye to Kevin, she presses something into his hand. A note. It reads:

<blockquote class="kg-blockquote-alt">OZ60wF</blockquote>

He isn’t quite sure what it means, but Denver takes priority over the mystery. He and Wren consider whether their riders can work together to help Denver in some way.

> Kevin: “I’m showing you how easy it is for you to destroy. I challenge you to create.”

Kevin acknowledges that his rider doesn’t necessarily play well with others, but his destructive tendencies may have a productive use here. Whiro, devourer of worlds, can consume the darkness inside Denver while Sekhmet and Wren protect the soul they are trying to save. Kevin squeezes Denver’s hand, reaches for Wren’s, and Stops Holding Back, letting Whiro work through him in a way he has never allowed before. As the magic rises in the room, he feels Denver ever so softly squeeze his hand back.

## Helix Labs
> Michelle: “Did you have a nice vacation in which you became wanted by the police?”

Sergei sneaks into Helix through a freight entrance and goes to his office. Michelle appears almost immediately, ready for anything: she has a heavy length of pipe in case he’s an intruder, and a hot cup of coffee in case he’s Sergei. 

> Michelle: “There are many things that we do for good reasons that should not be illegal as scientists.”

She hands him the coffee and explains that she knows about the police, but isn’t going to turn him in. He admits that he has probably done at least some of the things he’s accused of, but that they were for good reasons and, you know, shouldn’t be illegal.

> Michelle: “Can I have a blood sample of yours?”<br />
Sergei: “I did not think that would be first question.”<br />
> Entire Table: “You know, there are other ways to get DNA samples, Sergei.”

*(I’m so proud of us for all making that joke at once. Really. We adult so well.)*

The revelation that Sergei is John’s clone produces considerable scientific interest, some examination of his hair, and an offer to study him in greater detail. *(Sergei continues to understand this primarily as a research proposal.)* Before explaining much more about what has been happening, he frets about corporate surveillance, and Michelle acknowledges everything is recorded. He turns on the water in his lab sink, borrowing a precaution from an earlier conversation involving Maeve and three guys in a bathroom.

> GalleryCillian: “I was like, ‘Oh shit, we’re doing what now? I gotta get laid, man.’ …That whole thing was rough.”

He tells Michelle about Armand’s enslaved workers and the threat to his friends. She knows there have been labs beneath the City, though Sergei does not disclose the Sunken City itself. His immediate project is the **Mythos multimeter**, intended to measure the differences between divine and folkloric Rifts and examine how the collars respond to them. Between a still-functional collar and a Men in Gray bullet, he has material to work with; what he needs is a way to protect his divine friends from whatever Armand is preparing. Michelle agrees to help and moves in beside him to look at his work. Sergei notices that she smells nice…

## Avalon (The Griffin’s Nest)
> Cillian: “How could I not notice that the weather is different?”<br />
> Maeve: “You called it a microclimate.”

Quilla’s directions get Maeve and Cillian safely to Avalon. He can finally perceive the place properly, which just leads to more questions. Cillian braces himself for the meeting with Cyrus, unsure what to expect, but their reunion ends with a genuine-if-stiff hug rather than a confrontation as Cillian slowly finds his footing. Maeve launches herself at Cyrus, hugging him and babbling heartfelt thanks for helping Geoffrey and Daniel.

They settle in with beers and begin to unravel the mystery. Cillian reveals to Maeve that his mother, Shifra, runs the Irish mob, which is largely an assassin organization. She operates out of Parkside. Cyrus fills in that she leads the supernatural Wild Hunt that results in a rash of murders in The City once a year. Shifra has known for years that Cillian is foretold to succeed her as the *McCaireach*, leader of all the *Tuatha Dé Danann* forces in The City — more commonly called the fae.

<Callout color="blue" emoji="🌿">
*McCaireach* means *Son of Cailleach*. The *Cailleach* is a legendary divine hag in Gaelic myth. 
</Callout>

Cillian’s powers first began to emerge at 19 when Petra, the love of his life, died in a "car accident." Everyone knows it was, in fact, murder, but there's no proof. His mother disapproved of the relationship and forcibly ended it. When Cillian’s grief and rage began to awaken his powers, she had an accomplice place a geas on him rather than surrender her position to her foretold successor.

> GalleryWren: “You banged this box open?”<br />
> Maeve: “Apparently, that’s how it works.”

The binding on his magic would hold until Cillian could be with someone without thinking of Petra. The night he and Maeve shared in the Sunken City apparently met that condition, which explains why her perception of his Mythos changed from one day to the next. Whatever was trapped in that “locked box” is free for the first time in over ten years.

> Cyrus: “The *McCaireach* is in charge of the fae in the city, making sure that… well, if you're Shifra, it's about doing what you want. If it's someone more humane, it's about keeping them in check.”

Cyrus believes other fae would support Cillian if he takes a stand, but his mother is feared, effective, and well connected. She knows Armand; Hades is an enemy, and their relationship is why Cillian can’t easily go to Ocean Heights. Cillian asks about Seamus and his loyalties. Cyrus tells him that Seamus isn’t just a fae; he’s one of the fae kings, and his redcap associates with their blood-soaked hats rule the docks at night. He may support Cillian’s claim, but Cyrus freely admits that getting him to agree to dismantling the assassins’ operation would be another matter.

<Callout color="blue" emoji="🌿">
    Cillian's mother, Shifra runs the Irish mob out of Parkside, oversees its assassination operation, and holds authority over the City’s fae -- a position Cillian is foretold to inherit. Now he gets to decide what he wants to do about it.
</Callout>

Maeve asks what Fiona’s role in the organization is or was. Cillian tersely explains that she was one of his mother’s enforcers, useful until she wasn’t and subsequently dismissed. Maeve accidentally steps on a landmine, revealing that Something Happened between Fiona and Cillian when he was fifteen and she was twenty-two, and Cillian draws a firm boundary that this isn’t up for further discussion. Maeve apologizes for digging *(and starts plotting how to plunge Fiona into a nightmare she can never escape)*.

> Maeve: “I have an apartment, if you don't care about ghosts or hot water…”

Shifra knows the geas is broken, which means she’s declared war on Cillian. He can’t return to Parkside, and his financial resources have been cut off. By association, Maeve may be in danger as well. Petra was not Cillian’s only girlfriend to meet a violent end. Maeve offers her place before Cyrus reminds her she can’t go home either. She starts fretting about getting word to Gladys, and Cyrus offers to send someone to speak with her. 

> Cyrus: “You have my sword to protect you, McCaireach. I am—“<br />
> Maeve: “But you're not even related to the fae. Why do you care?”<br />
> Cyrus: “Of course I'm related to the fae. They are the ones who provided me with my sword.”<br />
>Maeve: “Oh right, watery tarts, what give swords to people. Got it.”

Cillian cannot solve his family drama tonight, but he can address their more pressing situation which may involve steep consequences for both Maeve and himself. They bring Cyrus up to speed on what’s happening, and the potential danger to all divine Mythics in The City.

> Maeve: “Just because one of my eyes turns black does not mean I’m on Team Black Eyes.”<br />
> Cillian: “You’re scary as shit and I love it.”

Maeve starts to spiral as they discuss how to fight an unspecified supernatural catastrophe. There will presumably be a spooky ritual thing, it’ll be some Buffy shit, some sort of circle, possibly salt, everybody’s eyes turning black, and then they probably all die, but nobody knows how… Cillian kisses her, rerouting her train of thought. He then asks Cyrus for safe passage back to the others, and his friend promises help when they need it.

## Passing Notes
> GalleryKevin: “B assumed we wouldn’t roll snake eyes.”

Kevin has a message to decipher: **OZ60wF.** Parts of it are easy. Old Zion is the major church and community institution in Independence. 60 is probably a time, one hour from now. The last part defeats both Kevin and Wren despite B’s apparent confidence that handing over the note would be all they needed.

Kevin calls London from the office phone, jumping through layers of identification and misdirection before London trusts that it is actually him. He gives London the mysterious code, which he then takes to Daniel. He searches for patterns, leaning on both his powers and his tablet. Daniel reveals the deep hidden meaning: the remaining letters mean **with Friends**. Bring everyone. Kevin immediately reaches out to the whole crew alerting them to the time and place.

> Kevin: “Maybe the brink sent her back. We gotta go.”

Before they leave Sweet Chariot, Kevin checks on Denver. She is still asleep, but the nurses are astonished: a woman who was on the brink is now sleeping peacefully. Doctors come in to look and marvel. Kevin keeps the truth to himself, suggesting some hosannas, agreeing that it is a miracle, and gets moving.

## Avengers, Assemble
John asks Quilla and Allison about transport for those at the safe house and sends the meeting details to Sergei. 

At Helix Labs, the research is going well enough that Sergei can leave it for now, while Michelle continues to work, helping to protect his friends. 

> Cyrus: “Cillian, you gotta watch out for this one if she's friends with Isabella Aguilar’s drummer.”

Maeve and Cillian get the summons, too, and Cyrus is suitably impressed that his “higher up’s got friends in high places.” He supplies motorcycles and helmets, much to Maeve’s delight, though her confidence that letting her drive one will be *totally fine* does not persuade Cillian to hand over the keys. She rides behind him *(not a hardship)*, and he promises to teach her later.

As Maeve and Cillian speed through Whitecliff, the scattered crew has an overdue conversation about who knows what. Cyrus, Maria, and Yasmin all know the larger threat; London deliberately withheld it from Marcus, and nobody else filled him in. Geoffrey proposes updating Alma through the tattoo-shop network about Erica, including where she is. London automatically objects to passing that location around; a network under pressure is not automatically a safe network.

## Old Zion Church
![](/assets/images/2026/11/zion.png)

As the group trickles in, they are greeted by an ancient woman with long cornrows and a warm, wide smile. She introduces herself as Olivia. Old Zion has grown around its community, acquiring additions and useful spaces without acquiring much wealth. She recognizes Kevin as a local and hopes he’ll come back another time. Olivia is retiring, and she introduces her smiling successor as de León. B’s guests are directed to the choir rehearsal room.

The surname troubles London, and the crew kicks around the possibility that it’s not a coincidence *(it is)*. Geoffrey points out that it is a common name; Daniel tries to glean information by looking through the mist, but comes up empty-handed.

> Daniel: “Careful, uncertain footing in these old churches.”

London makes a caustic remark about Daniel failing to see things in front of his face. Shortly thereafter, London’s feet “accidentally” go out from under him as he slips on a random wet patch of flooring, while Kevin and Wren hear the commotion as they join the group. Daniel smiles smugly.

Maeve and Cillian arrive, windblown and looking sufficiently flattened by their afternoon that Geoffrey sidles over to ask what happened. Before that conversation develops, B emerges from the shadows. She is glad they are alive and apologizes for leaving them stranded alone in the water during their earlier escape, which was not her plan. 

B promises she will not turn them over to the authorities. She has her own history with Armand and is proud of the things she has done against him. There are still people trapped in his enclave,  though, and she and her friends have a plan to get them out.

## The Grand Adventure
> B: “Slavery in **any** form is not something that I am willing to sit by and do nothing about.”

Maeve asks whether B knows about the larger threat to divine Rifts. She explains the Tezcatlipocas and the fourth one they have kept out of Armand’s hands—for now. Erica is badly injured and under medical care, which makes her vulnerable. They still don’t know where the other three are held: they weren’t in the cells with Kevin and Wren, and the crew did not conduct a complete search of the hacienda.

B’s plan has two parts. Tomorrow is Friday, with protests planned across the City and an Isabella Aguilar concert drawing a substantial crowd. This is the benefit where John is playing, wanted man or not. B expects Armand’s police and other controlled personnel to be occupied suppressing the unrest around the City, giving an extraction team its opening. They will enter through the barn and free the enslaved people inside the enclave. B says she can get them through the barrier but does not explain how; the barn isn’t the only entrance, just the one they intend to use.

They expect Armand will be inside the enclave and Duke will be at the concert. John is asked to make the largest distraction he can onstage to keep Armand's forces there occupied. Daniel probes for more information, wanting to understand the risk before agreeing, and B makes it clear that they could lose everything, including their lives. Armand will not let this go quietly.

> Geoffrey: “Honey, this is why we’re here. The grand adventure. I would have thought that you'd changed perspective on all of this, that you'd be willing to jump in.”<br />
> Daniel: “Well, **I** thought you’d been expressing some concerns about my changing opinions on risk management.”

One by one, either enthusiastically or reluctantly, with baggage or without, the crew agrees to B’s plan. And they get to work.
