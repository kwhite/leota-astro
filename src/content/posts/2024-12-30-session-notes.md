### The Drum

<figure class="kg-card kg-image-card kg-card-hascaption"><a href="/assets/images/2025/01/89B1E8DF-E9B3-4D66-8807-44F4DA5AA5F3.png"><img src="/assets/images/2025/01/89B1E8DF-E9B3-4D66-8807-44F4DA5AA5F3.png" class="kg-image" alt="" loading="lazy" width="1024" height="1024"></a><figcaption>Jar we extracted from the small hand drum.</figcaption></figure>

- Looking at the drum metaphysically

  - It is *fucked.*

  - Kevin and London both see brooms spinning out of control around a young man in a magician’s robe.

  - The drum is acting like an Artifact with magic attached to it — like an enclave, but this is an object, a Relic.
  - Magic is not emanating from the drum, but instead within the drum
  - Drum is a “cage” for what’s producing the magic.
- London takes the drum around the corner while the rest of the party goes to the tea shop; leaves Xian-Gang with Sergei and his cat.

  - Slices it open and finds a glass jar inside.
  - The jar is sealed with a clasp-like stopper and contains a thick, black, glowing liquid. The same brooms spin around the jar that we noted when examining the drum.
  - This jar does not resemble anything they recognize.

  - Chalchiuhtotolin thinks this is amateurish work that is beneath their notice. *(He is so judgy.)*

<figure class="kg-card kg-image-card kg-card-hascaption"><a href="/assets/images/2025/01/IMG_0436.jpeg"><img src="/assets/images/2025/01/IMG_0436.jpeg" class="kg-image" alt="" loading="lazy" width="800" height="942"></a><figcaption>The Mysterious Michele</figcaption></figure>
