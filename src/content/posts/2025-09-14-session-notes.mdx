### The fight

- There’s a fight in the bazaar. A really big, really bad, really long fight.

- Ferric falls and is all-dead (not mostly dead) after a coup-de-grace. His body is tucked under a tent near Phelan in the final fight position.

- Valgara and Phelan are both badly hurt and in melee fighting back to back, surrounded by enemies (an Erinyes, two Bearded Devils, and a Chain Devil).
- Erash falls to the horned devil, but gets back up on his feet with 2 HP thanks to Snow.
- Snow maintains her distance and is providing ranged support, slinging spells and arrows.
