### Scene investigation

- Tire tracks that end abruptly in the yard.
- Footprints that continue around the back side of the building, multiple sets; seems to be a high traffic pathway.
- In the back, there are bags of Candy (see below).
- There’s a door back here (that we didn’t try).
- The interior looks completely abandoned, with some graffiti on the walls and a general dusty, undisturbed air.
- The basement area has a tangled network of pipes and an antique piece of factory machinery spattered with equally antique bloodstains. But there‘s no sign of recent activity.
- There’s no way that the outfit that Briana told the team about could have vacated the premises as recently as 10 minutes ago.
- Based on what we’ve experienced with Briana and the other girl, the Furnace seems to be a one-way exit.

### Enclaves

- Enclaves are places that exist outside of the physical world.
- They are usually controlled spaces that are impossible to breach.
- To enter, one needs permission from the controlling party or some kind of key to get in.
- Example: Gateway Tunnels in La Colonia. Successfully accessing the many doors and branches of these tunnels requires a certain attitude or mental state.
