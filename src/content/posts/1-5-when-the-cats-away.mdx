## Red Yarn 🧶

### Apples + Oranges (A+O)

- **Apples and Oranges (A+O)** is a big agriculture company providing fresh produce, meat, and dairy to The City.
- They have a reputation for shady labor practices.
- **Armand Short** is the owner of A+O, and the father of **Briana Short**.
- Short has a vested interest in the whereabouts of **Erica Garza**, who has gone missing. Per Short's fixer, **David Orlov,** Garza has worked with them sporadically in the past and is critical to moving an undisclosed important project forward.
- A+O runs meatpacking facilities in La Colonia. They own a bunch of shell corporations that enable them to operate additional factories with lower safety and working standards.
- A+O facilities had a string of deaths that were too numerous to be accidental. We have not explored these deaths further.
- **Rogelio Lopez** tried to blow the whistle on the A+O labor practices, but the A+O legal team exposed his crimes perpetrated by El Cuyo, his Rift, discrediting him as a witness. A+O hired him back at another plant under one of these shell corporations.
- A+O owns soup kitchens in lower-income areas, including La Colonia and Independence.

### The Saint Within

- There are volunteers at the A+O soup kitchens, at least in La Colonia, who are representatives of an organization called ***The Saint Within***. These volunteers are able to identify and talk to people with Rifts and offer them a way to remove them.
- We know that those who come out of the program at *The Saint Within* have a "glow" about them, but their Rifts appear to truly be gone.

### Alex Duke

- **Alex Duke** is a disgraced cop who should have been kicked off the force but wasn't after he murdered somebody in La Colonia. He's not allowed in any official capacity there or in Independence.
- He is good looking in a blonde "Ken Doll" kind of way.
- Duke is still frequently seen in La Colonia because he moonlights in private security and is often spotted near A+O properties.
- Alex Duke has abducted at least two of the four Aztec creator gods, the **Tezcatlipocas**.

### The Men in Gray/Shrouders

- The **Men in Gray** have an implied mission of ridding The City of Rifts before these gods-made-flesh come and destroy everything and everyone in their pursuit of their individual goals. **Ville Haavikko** is one of their agents, and a Shrouder, who are a subset of the organization.
- **Shrouders** are responsible for evaluating people for their “use to society” in a currently unknown context.
- **Briana Short** has ties to the men in gray, and is also against her father's labor practices.
- We know that it's possible to distill a Rift and make it usable by anyone who has ownership of the resulting substance.

<Callout color="blue" emoji="👻">

We thought this capability died with the candy witch's oven, but there may be other ways to achieve the same goal.

- The **Niners** in Independence round up "troublemakers" in that district and take them to City Hall, where they are evaluated by the Shrouders.
- The prisoners in City Hall are of all ages, but are almost exclusively racial minorities.
- Both **Denver Morrison** and **Inez Hill** show signs of some kind of metaphysical tampering, which manifests as "stitches" as though some part of them has been excised and stitched back together. Denver shows multiple instances of this.
- Both Denver and Inez have had run-ins with the Niners in Independence.
- Per **B**, some of the prisoners that the Shrouders identify as "useful" are transported to another location – a farming Enclave at a barn in the southwest part of town.
