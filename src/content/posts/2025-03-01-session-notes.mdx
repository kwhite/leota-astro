## The Plumgroves

- When we got to the Plumgroves, there were more guards than usual, being more vigilant than usual, but they hadn’t seen him.
- Ultimately we tracked Thar to a makeshift camp full of caged prisoners (he was one of them). There was a magic dude in charge who was a prick and was prone to monologuing. He had an equally unpleasant gray render companion.
- We had a big fight, after which we established the mercenaries were being paid in Kryn gold.
- There was an older gentleman among the prisoners who had seen the text on Erash’s tattoo before, somewhere in the ruins of Draconia...
