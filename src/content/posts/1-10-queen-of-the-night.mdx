## When last we met...

Having decided that helping Catherine take over the Sunken City was an ill-advised idea, the crew arranged an exit instead. Agent Dire—a stranded Man in Gray connected to the Niner cloning program—showed them the way into the Undertrash in exchange for Maeve delivering a letter to his wife and daughter. Sergei left with a completed ray gun and a phone that could reach Daedalus from the surface.

Their departure did not go unnoticed. With Catherine, Rasputin, and guards in pursuit, Maeve unleashed a nightmare and Wren set the entrance ablaze. They escaped through the tunnels, losing London’s horse and Kevin’s tuatara at the boundary where the Mist changed.

Now they are in the Undertrash, domain of the Queen of the Night. Still underground, still separated from Geoffrey and Daniel, and very ready to go home.

## Under the City

The cave out of the Sunken City becomes more urban and increasingly filthy, and the stench of trash becomes almost overpowering. With everyone’s mythoi back to something approaching normal, the crew picks through the dark by phone light. Wren’s earlier decision to put the phones in rice has paid off. Even London’s mostly works, although given his relationship with technology, it may take him a while to notice the difference.

A break in the concrete overhead offers a possible way up. Maeve asks Cillian whether he can Jack-and-the-Beanstalk them out of here, but apparently, that is no longer an option. He seems as surprised as she is. When she takes a closer look, she can still detect something supernatural in him; whatever happened below has not completely gone away. But it's definitely not as powerful here.

Which means that for now, all they have to work with is a very tall cop and a walking stick.

Kevin climbs onto Cillian’s shoulders and peeks through the broken concrete, finding an abandoned subway tunnel above them. He wedges the sturdy walking stick he has scavenged into the rubble as a handhold, although the maneuver also earns Cillian a Birkenstock to the face. John follows, receives a very specific warning about kicking Cillian, kicks him anyway, and gets punched in the balls. Catherine’s hopes for grandchildren suffer another setback.

As their phones regain reception, messages from Geoffrey and Daniel arrive in a flood. They are alive and have been hiding out at the Griffin’s Nest for several days, trying to keep Geoffrey from being arrested.

## Next Level

Through another break in the floor, Cillian has found a brightly lit cavern. Below them is an enormous winding earthen mound, with greenery and none of the immediate station’s accumulated filth.

Daniel looks down and jumps.

Cillian follows rather than leave him down there alone. Kevin and John go after them. Geoffrey, still catching up with Maeve, now has to explain that this is precisely the problem.

When he washed up after the river, the mermaid, Maya, thought the others had gone down the whirlpool and were probably dead. Geoffrey refused to believe her. He felt he would have known. He made it home, only to discover that Daniel had become someone who cheerfully walks into danger and dismisses every attempt to talk about it. Geoffrey can still recognize his husband, but the familiar caution is missing.

Maeve tells him something of what the Sunken City did to their own powers, including Cillian’s awakening. *(She glosses over the fact that she did indeed die in that whirlpool.)* But that does not explain Daniel, who was not there with them – and they are back to normal now anyway. As normal as they get, at least.

On the lower level, people have tucked tents into the curves of the giant mound, and flickering shadows from cook fires dance over its high, sinuous shape. A man cooking a hot dog over a fire introduces himself as Gib. Daniel asks where they can find the Queen of the Night, and Gib suggests the Night Court. Daniel explains that he needs to get a message to someone on the surface. They talk a bit about Yasmin, but Gib doesn't have much information. He’s only seen her from a distance. Gib's clothes are worn and a little dirty, but recognizably modern.

There is no obvious source for the cavern’s light. John gets a sense of something ancient here, something full of potential, although he cannot yet say what.

Back up above, London finally interrupts Maeve and Geoffrey to point out that their companions have disappeared. They descend to catch up.

Daniel has already found something else.

## The Settlement

Unnaturally colored smoke wafts out from one of the tents. Daniel approaches just as its flap opens and a man in a gray fedora steps out. Startled, the man draws a gun.

Daniel looks... amused?!? He tilts the probabilities toward a misfire, and the gun clicks uselessly.

> Daniel: “I don’t think that’s gonna work.”

Then a wave of dizziness hits him. His connection to his Mythos drops away.

Cillian calls out the gun, and Geoffrey grabs Daniel and yanks him aside. Daniel continues laughing, but the laughter is becoming ragged. His certainty has gone.

Cillian catches the gunman’s arm, and John helps wrestle the weapon away, jamming a finger in the process. Another man emerges from the tent with another gun. Then another.

London moves around the side of the tent, draws his switchblade, and starts cutting ropes while Kevin works on pulling up the stakes as the two try to collapse the tent. Neither gets it down immediately. Out front, the second gunman fires at John, who pulls the initial gunman partly in front of himself as a shield. The protection is incomplete: John is left with his Mythos impaired from the shot.

Sergei brings out the weapon he developed to affect crystalline orichalcum. His first attempt goes badly, hitting the tent and leaving him briefly blinded.

Maeve, still without the gun Duke’s people took from her, checks for ghosts in the area. There are a *lot* of them down here.

She manages to force four of them to follow her instructions. She assigns one to closing the zipper on the tent, one to help pull tent stakes, and one to stick a finger into a gun barrel, Looney Tunes style. The last one she orders to enter the tent and "poltergeist shit up."

To anyone who can see what she is doing, Maeve takes on the divided aspect of her Mythos: moonlight and shadow, one eye glowing white and one obsidian black, cold air stirring her hair. Her voice becomes strangely alien as she gives the ghosts their commands.

“Is it weird that you’re hot that way?” Cillian asks, still grappling with a gunman.

The tent zips shut. There is eventually screaming from inside.

Geoffrey is still holding Daniel, whose attention has returned to the smoke. He stops struggling, and when he speaks again, he sounds more like himself. He tells Geoffrey they need to get away from the smoke, now.

## Queen of the Night

> **GalleryJohn:** “The enemy of my enemy is my friend.”<br />**GalleryMaeve:** “She’s totally our friend. Death goddesses are awesome.”<br />**GalleryKevin:** “She looks friend-shaped.”

There is shouting elsewhere in the cavern. John rallies the residents for help, putting the Pied Piper behind the appeal. People emerge from their tents and begin picking up sticks, hot-dog forks, and whatever else they can use.

More gray-clad figures are climbing up onto the top of the mound. On another part of it, a woman appears. London thinks he recognizes Ereshkigal, the Sumerian goddess of the dead. The Queen of the Night.

Below at the tent, Wren punches one of the armed men hard enough to stagger him, but he remains standing and turns his gun toward her. Kevin’s tent stake finally comes loose. He stumbles back, the stake flies, and it hits the man in the head. That finishes the job, and he crumples to the ground.
