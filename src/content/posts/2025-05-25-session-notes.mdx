## The City of Brass – *(a.k.a. Phelan's Lecture of the Week)*

> *“If the heat doesn’t melt your boots,<br />the politics surely will.”*

*— Chapter 3: On Avoiding Eye Contact with Efreeti Nobility, “Embers on the Horizon: A Gentleman’s Gallivant Through the City of Brass”, by Sir Thaddeus Quillfeather, Third Scion of the Smokewind Expeditionary Society*

### The City Environs

- The City floats on a disc that hovers over or on the Sea of Fire (it can be raised and lowered by the sultan depending on whether the ports are in use).
- The City is ruled by the ancient Efreeti Sultan Marrake al-Sidan al-Hariq ben Lazan.
- The Sultan rules from The Charcoal Palace, and has a legendary vault beneath the palace filled with unimaginable riches. Nobody has ever broken in or gotten out. It was built by the Azers.
- The City deals extensively in the slave trade, and crimes are frequently punished with slavery – even very minor infractions.
- The City welcomes a lot of trade, so the Grand Sultan keeps the temperature cool enough to accommodate visitors during the day (about 80ºF).
- There's a break from 14:00 to 17:00 called qayloulah *(kay-LOO-lah)* where the temperature is increased back to it's natural state, which is around 150º F. At night time, the temperature is at this same extreme or hotter.
- Water is scarce but more available in the city than elsewhere on the Plane of Fire. It's not cheap (think wine pricing), but you can get it.

### The Caste System

- The City operates with a very strict caste system.
- The City is ruled by the Efreeti nobility; following them in the class structure are common Efreeti, free fire plane natives, and then slaves.
- Travelers from other planes, being "casteless," are ranked separately within the city's rigid hierarchy. Basically, your wealth and/or power (on display) determine your value and the level of attention you merit.
- Poor or unprotected travelers generally wind up as slaves.

### Planar Geology &amp; Access

- The further you go into the plane, the more primordial it becomes. The City of Brass is located on the outer edges of the plane.
- Access is typically from places in your home plane that are the closest to a pure elemental form; lava floes, volcanoes, and other sources of strong geothermic activity.
- Possible Wildemount Access Points:

  - The Maker's Observatory *(has been destroyed)*
  - The River Inferno *(Eiselcross, really really far and dangerous)*
  - Everplume *(underwater volcano NW of Port Demali – hag territory)*
  - Kravaraad *(north of Uthodurn)*
  - **Mt. Mentiri *(Kamordah, near Deastock)***
