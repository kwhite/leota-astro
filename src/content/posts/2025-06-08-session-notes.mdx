### Vallah’s Insights

<figure class="kg-card kg-image-card kg-card-hascaption"><a href="/assets/images/2025/06/IMG_0612.png"><img src="/assets/images/2025/06/IMG_0612.png" class="kg-image" alt="" loading="lazy" width="928" height="1232" /></a><figcaption>Vallah of the Forge</figcaption></figure>

- As far as she knows, the Effriti are not aware of Erash’s existence.
- He has allies in the oppressed races of the fire plane; the Azers, the elementals and others will help him if he asks for aid.
- The fire giants are less predictable. Some of them have sold their allegiance to the Sultan. But some would stand with him.
- The closest portal to the Plane of Fire is in **Vuthos**, the fire giant city underneath Mt. Mentiri.
- The leader of Vuthos, **Faz’hul al-Tasim**, was banned from the City of Brass.
- He now serves as the gatekeeper of access to the plane.
- Nobody enters the portal except by his permission; so our best chance of success is to sneak into Vuthos and slip through the portal undetected.
- Al-Tasim will be too proud to send forces after us if we are successful.
- Her advice: DON’T walk through the front door of the City of Brass OR Vuthos and DON’T let them know who Erash really is.
- Fanfare is not our friend; it is best to NOT attract notice.

### Vallah’s Story

- Vallah and her family fled The Plane of Fire to escape the oppression under the Sultan.
- She and her mother escaped; but her father did not make it.
- Her mother died of her wounds shortly afterwards.

### On the Nature of Dragons *(a.k.a. Phelan’s Lecture of the Week)*

<Callout color="yellow" emoji="💡">

FYI, Kat does NOT know the endgame; this is her theory based on common dragon characteristics.

</Callout>

- Generally, Dragons, specifically Red Dragons, care the most about treasure hoarding, being treated and tithed to like a god, or some combination of the two.
- Based on the story we heard about the origins of Erash’s magical bloodline, it was *Erash’s ancestor* who wanted to overthrow the Sultan. So Gaushroth gave him sorcerous power in exchange for something.
- We have no idea what that something is, but if Phelan had to *guess*, it has nothing to with the City of Brass itself or wanting to rule it.
- But it probably *does* have to do with that room in Erash’s dragon-y visions that only he seems to be able to access.
- So we need to go, find the room, and figure out how we either fulfill the deal and get everyone off our back or … renegotiate terms. Or something.
