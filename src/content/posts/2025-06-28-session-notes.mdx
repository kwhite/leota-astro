> We are just trying to get ourselves killed in the City of Brass. Could you just... let us do that? <br />- Snow

## Outskirts of Vuthos

- Crew strategizes at length about how to approach the fire plane portal.
- Determine that more reconnaisance is needed; Khoraka volunteers.
- He heads out in a flurry of spell casting, and says that if he's gone more than 7 minutes, we should assume something happened.
- Khoraka and the DM go to the office with their dice. Oh dear...

## The Search for Khoraka

- Ten minutes later, we're pretty sure something happened.
- Figuring there's nothing else for it, the party decides to approach the military barracks since it has the only non-gated entry point to the city.
- Erash hides in Snow's Handy Haversack, Phelan goes to wolf form, Valgara keeps her ears open for giants talking, and we spell up.
- SO. MANY. STEALTH. CHECKS. 🥃
- Snow makes for the bridge and is stunned by the watchtower guards. Alarms go off.
- Fire giants swarm the area.
- Phelan runs to Snow's (and Erash's) rescue. They are detained in a force cage.
- Ferric and Valgara are also captured.
- The group is forcibly taken to the prisons.

## The Prison

- ... where we find Khoraka! Yay for that!
- We have all our stuff, but magic doesn't work and our primary weapons are missing.
- The unexpectedly friendly guard, Snarg, feeds us very well and explains that we're to compete in coliseum fights in the morning.
- We sleep.
